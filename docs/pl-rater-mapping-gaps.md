# PL Rater mappings and CRM gaps

Audit: 2026-11-24, Tracey Dittrich's existing Minnesota package. The complete control manifest is `public/mappings/quotecraftz-pl-rater.json`, regenerated by `node scripts/build-pl-rater-mappings.mjs`. It contains mapping metadata, not captured customer values. Secured targets remain local-only.

Covered screens: Client Information; Auto General, Vehicles, Additional Interest, Drivers, Incidents and Company Questions; Home General, Property, Additional Interest, Coverage, Additional Information, Loss and Company Questions. The 32 Home optional coverage sections and their available detail controls were inspected individually. Empty inspection rows were cancelled. No quote was rated, bound, purchased or submitted.

Primary client and driver 1 map to the primary contact. Drivers 2 and 3 map to related contacts 1 and 2. Vehicles retain distinct rows 1–3; previous/mailing addresses use customer-address rows 2/3; property data uses property row 1. This manifest covers the audited package, not unvisited products, states, carriers, extra rows or future layouts.

## Choices, security and exclusions

Explicit choice translations: Work → Commute; Leasing Company → Lessor; all ten education levels; Undergraduate → College Student; hip-roof variants → Hip; In-Ground → In Ground. Original source choices are also retained in scoped notes alongside coarser CRM classifications. Unsupported indoor-pool/construction variants remain recoverable. Placeholders are not answers.

Billing/payment and carrier agent-identity controls are Never fill. Derived driver age and years licensed are Never fill because DOB and licensing date are separate inputs. Prospect/Client does not control CRM lead qualification. SSN/license numbers use secured pass-through, never notes or repository data.

Import the manifest through the site's mapping import in extension Settings. Import retains other sites and local secured mappings. Nine secured client/driver bindings were taught through the live picker; Elizabeth explicitly uses related-contact row 2. Vehicle-note writes require a successfully loaded matching vehicle and fail visibly instead of falling back to an account/property.

PL Rater's existing package has Protective Devices enabled without individual devices selected. It can show a required-information dialog on navigation. Do not fabricate device answers to bypass it; inspect the controls and return to the existing quote without rating it.
